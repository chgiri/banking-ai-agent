Personal Loan FAQs

Q: What is the minimum credit score required?
A: A minimum credit score of 650 is required for personal loan eligibility.

Q: What is the maximum loan tenure?
A: Personal loans can be availed for a tenure of 12 to 60 months.

Q: Is there a prepayment penalty?
A: Yes, a prepayment penalty of 2% on the outstanding principal applies if the loan is closed within the first 12 months. No penalty applies after 12 months.

Q: Can I get a loan with a co-applicant?
A: Yes, adding a co-applicant with a stable income can improve eligibility and may result in a better interest rate.
