Standard Account Fee Schedule

- Monthly minimum balance requirement: $500 for Savings, $1000 for Current accounts
- Non-maintenance penalty: $15/month if balance falls below minimum
- ATM withdrawal (own bank): Free, unlimited
- ATM withdrawal (other bank): First 3 free per month, $2.50 thereafter
- International transaction fee: 3% of transaction amount
- Card replacement fee: $10 for standard, free for damaged chip issues
- Stop payment on cheque: $25 per request
